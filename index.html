Four quick steps, no software to install and no account to create.

What does the tool actually remove?

Images made with Gemini usually carry a small four-point sparkle in the bottom-right corner, and some AI videos carry a similar mark. This tool covers that visible logo and rebuilds the pixels underneath from the area around it, so you don't end up with a blurry blob or an empty box.

On smooth backgrounds like skies, gradients and studio shots the patch is practically invisible. On very detailed textures you may notice a faint smooth spot. A little extra edge padding or a second pass usually sorts that out.

One honest note: this only removes the visible logo. Google also embeds an invisible SynthID signal in Gemini output, and this tool doesn't touch it. If an image is AI-generated and that matters to your audience, say so.

Most people use it for slides, mockups, thumbnails, social posts and print layouts built from their own generations. Only edit files you created or have the right to change, and check Google's terms for the way you plan to use them.

Tips for a clean result

  • Start from the original download, not a screenshot. Screenshots shift the logo and add compression noise.
  • Save as PNG if you plan to edit again. JPG and WebP are re-encoded every time you save.
  • See a faint outline? Raise the edge padding by 2 to 4 px.
  • Logo on a white background? Auto-detect may miss it. Draw the box yourself, it works the same.
  • For video, keep the box tight. A smaller box gives a cleaner fill and a faster export.
  • Several marks? Press Add area and repeat.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about privacy, formats and what to expect.

Is the Gemini watermark remover really free?

Yes. There's no sign-up, no limit on how many files you process, and no watermark added to your results.

Are my images and videos uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens in your browser, on your own device. Your files are never sent to a server, which also means the tool keeps working offline once the page has loaded.

Does it remove SynthID or other invisible watermarks?

No. It only removes the visible sparkle logo. Invisible signals such as SynthID stay in the file, and this tool doesn't try to change them.

Which file types does it support?

Images can be PNG, JPG or WebP. For video, any format your browser can play works, such as MP4, WebM or MOV in Safari. Videos are saved as MP4 or WebM, depending on your browser.

Why wasn't the sparkle found automatically?

Auto-detect looks for the sparkle in the bottom-right corner at the sizes Gemini uses. Screenshots, crops, resized images and logos on white backgrounds can throw it off. Just drag a box over the logo and let go; the result updates instantly.

Will the image quality get worse?

Only the small area around the logo is rebuilt, and everything else keeps its original pixels. PNG is lossless. JPG and WebP downloads are saved at high quality (95%).

Why does exporting a video take as long as the clip?

The video is processed frame by frame in your browser and recorded in real time, which is what keeps it private. A 30-second clip takes about 30 seconds. Keep the tab open and visible while it runs.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The tool works on phones, tablets and desktops, and you can install it to your home screen like an app. Long videos are easier on a computer.

Is it okay to remove the watermark?

That depends on where the image is used and what Google's terms say for your case. As a rule, only edit content you created or have permission to change, and be upfront about AI-generated work where it matters.
